NAACCR XML: Tumor.recurrenceDate1st

Alternate Names
   Recurrence Date--1st
   Date of First Recurrence (CoC)
Description

Records the date of the follow up of the patient’s disease status or date of documentation of the patient’s first recurrence or progression of disease. Formerly, Recurrence Date--1st.

Rationale

This data item is used to measure the efficacy of the first course of treatment and patient disease status follow up.
