NAACCR XML: Tumor.cancerStatusFollowing1stCrsTx

Description

Records the presence or absence of evidence of the patient’s disease following first course treatment and corresponds with the Date of Cancer Status Following First Course of TX [1768] for cases diagnosed 2027 forward.

Rationale

This information is used to identify the cancer status of the patient following completion of first course treatment (not including long term maintenance therapy).

Refer to STORE Manual (2026+) for detailed coding instructions.

Codes
1No evidence of disease (NED)
2Evidence of disease
8First course treatment in progress
9Unknown, indeterminate whether this tumor is present; not stated in patient record
