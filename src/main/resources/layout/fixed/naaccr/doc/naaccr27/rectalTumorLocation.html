NAACCR XML: Tumor.rectalTumorLocation

Description

Location of the rectal cancer is important to document. Anteriorly, the peritoneal reflection is located at the junction of middle and lower third of the rectum, while laterally, it is located at the junction of upper and middle third of the rectum. Posteriorly, the reflection is located higher and most of the posterior rectum does not have a serosal covering.

Rationale

The location of rectal cancer in relation to peritoneal reflection and its distance from the anal verge is crucial in determining: 

  • Choice of neoadjuvant therapy: Tumors located below peritoneal reflection, particularly those with high-risk features, are recommended for TNT with systemic chemotherapy and chemoradiation to increase potential for clinical response and sphincter preservation.
  • Surgical planning: locally advanced rectal cancer involving the anorectal ring will require abdominoperineal resection (APR). Early rectal cancer below peritoneal reflection is amenable to trans-anal sphincter-preservation procedures without the need for laparotomy
  • Risk of Local Recurrence: tumors below the peritoneal reflection lack the serosal layer and have a higher risk of local recurrence and invasion of adjacent structures and pose surgical challenges and potentially affect bowel, bladder, and sexual function. 
  • Metastatic Potential and pattern of LN involvement
