NAACCR XML: Tumor.recurrenceType1st

Alternate Names
   Recurrence Type--1st
   Type of First Recurrence (CoC)
Description

Identifies the patient’s current disease status until first recurrence, progression or death. Once recurrence or progression occurs then identify the type of recurrence after a disease-free period or progression if the patient was never disease free. Formerly, Type of First Recurrence.

Rationale

This item is used to track the current status of the patient’s disease through progression or recurrence and helps evaluate treatment efficacy.

Codes
00Patient became disease-free after treatment and has not had a recurrence.
04_In situ_ recurrence of an invasive tumor.
06_In situ_ recurrence of an _in situ_ tumor.
10Local recurrence. Local recurrence includes recurrence confined to the remnant of the organ of origin, to the organ of origin, to the anastomosis, or to scar tissue where the organ previously existed. (This includes local recurrence of an in situ tumor and trocar recurrence.)
20Regional recurrence.
40Distant recurrence. Relapse of lymphoma, leukemia or other hematopoietic neoplasms.
70Patient has never been disease-free and there is no documentation of progression. This includes cases with distant metastasis at diagnosis, systemic disease, unknown primary, or minimal disease that is not treated.
80Patient has never been disease-free and has documentation of progression of disease.
88Disease has recurred, but the type of recurrence is unknown.
98Physician Documented Recurrence but collected as a new primary (_Solid Tumor Rules_)
99It is unknown whether the disease has recurred/progressed. Or first course treatment is still in progress (Cancer Status Code 8).
